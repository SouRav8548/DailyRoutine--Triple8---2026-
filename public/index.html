    Tips to make it work

    • Use the gaps between classes for self-study in the college library. That keeps evenings free.
    • Tuition is planned as 2 hours. If it runs to 2.5 hours, you lose only 30 minutes of free time, and the weekday self-study was already trimmed to make room for it.
    • Wednesday and Thursday are the heaviest days (labs plus tuition), so take the free morning slot to rest and sleep on time.
    • Put the phone away by 10:30 PM to fall asleep by 11:00.
    • Saturday evening is the main time for friends. Weekday evenings suit lighter hangouts or games.
    • Sunday stays a rest day apart from the two tuition sessions.
